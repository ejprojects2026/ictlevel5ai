What is database normalization?
Normalization organizes tables to reduce redundancy. It moves through normal forms: 1NF removes repeating groups, 2NF removes partial dependencies, 3NF removes transitive ones. Want a worked example? 📊
Fix my loop: for i in range(len(a)): print(a[i+1])
That throws an IndexError on the last item. Loop to len(a)-1, or just iterate directly: for x in a: print(x) ✅
